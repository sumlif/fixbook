PRIVACY

A small site.
A clear data trail.

Hosting

This site is designed for GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information when serving pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions and comments

Question links open GitHub Issues. Posts and account names are public. If embedded comments are enabled, they load through Utterances and GitHub; signing in is required only to post. Do not publish credentials, private messages or personal paths.

Optional traffic measurement

The blog includes an optional GoatCounter integration for per-page views and aggregate visitor/referrer trends. No analytics script loads until the site owner configures a valid endpoint. Browser blockers can prevent measurements; counts are not an exact record of every visitor. See GoatCounter’s privacy information.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 measures page views, sessions and interactions only after you accept Google Analytics cookies. Before acceptance, this site does not load the Google tag. Advertising storage, ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled in this implementation. Google may process browser, device and usage information after acceptance. See Google’s privacy policy.

Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. Use Analytics settings in the footer to change it. Rejecting Google Analytics does not disable the separate GoatCounter aggregate measurement described above. Browser blockers may also prevent data collection.

On this site

No advertising, newsletter signup or visitor account system is included. Search and category filtering run in your browser.
